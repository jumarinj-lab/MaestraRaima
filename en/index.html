Listen, understand, guide

A personal approach to every story

Every relationship and every personal concern has a different context. My consultations begin with listening, not with a predetermined ritual. You can explain what has happened, what you have tried and what you hope to understand.

After the initial assessment, I explain whether spiritual guidance may complement your next steps. I do not promise guaranteed outcomes, use fear to create urgency or present spiritual practices as a replacement for medical, psychological, legal or financial care.

Consultations are held virtually, making them available to clients in the United States and Colombia.

Responsible guidance

Respect comes before any ritual

Relationship services are approached through communication, harmony and personal clarity. They are not offered as a way to force, threaten or harm another person.

If a situation involves violence, stalking, coercion or immediate risk, contact qualified local support and emergency services. Spiritual guidance is not a substitute for safety measures.
